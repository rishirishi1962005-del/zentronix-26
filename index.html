// About the Symposium

About Zentronix

Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering

ZENTRONIX'26 is the annual national-level technical symposium organized by the Department of ECE. Like the Omnitrix unlocks alien abilities, this symposium unlocks the genius in every engineering mind — bringing together top talent from colleges across the nation.

Themed around the universe of Ben 10 this year, every event channels the power and personality of a unique alien — testing your intellect, creativity, and technical mastery in ways you've never experienced before.

Go hero. Compete across 7 power-packed events and prove your worth in the technological galaxy!
